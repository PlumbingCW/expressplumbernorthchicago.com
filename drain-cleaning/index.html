Emergency Drain Cleaning in North Chicago, IL

Water standing in a sink, a tub that will not empty, or a floor drain gurgling in the basement needs attention now. North Chicago Express Plumbing dispatches around the clock, 24/7. Until we arrive, stop running water, do not pour chemical cleaners down the drain, and keep a bucket handy if the fixture is close to overflowing.

We clear the blockage, then tell you what caused it, so you know whether it was a one-time clog or a sign of a pipe problem.

1

Which drain is clogged tells us where to look

One fixture and a whole house point to very different spots.

A single sink or tub that backs up usually has a clog in its own branch line, close to the fixture. If the toilet gurgles when you drain the washing machine, or the basement floor drain bubbles when someone showers upstairs, the blockage is probably farther down, in the main drain.

That one detail changes the tool we bring, the time the job takes, and the price. Tell us which fixtures are slow, which are stopped, and what sounds you hear. It is the fastest way to get the right equipment on the truck.

2

Kitchen sink clogs and what builds up in them

Kitchen sinks clog most often from grease that cooled and hardened inside the trap arm and the horizontal line behind the wall. Coffee grounds, rice, and fibrous peels add to it. Pouring hot water rarely helps, because the grease just moves down and cools again.

We use a drain cable sized to the pipe, run it past the trap arm, and pull the debris out instead of pushing it ahead. If the sink has a garbage disposal, we check that the disposal outlet is clear first. When the disposal itself is the problem, see our garbage disposal repair page.

Keep these out of the kitchen drain

  • ✓Cooking oil, bacon grease, and fat trimmings
  • ✓Coffee grounds and eggshells
  • ✓Rice, pasta, and potato peels
  • ✓Wipes labeled flushable, which are not drain safe
3

Tubs, showers, and bathroom sinks

Hair and soap scum form a mat just below the drain opening, and it can be pulled out when it is reachable. When the clog is deeper, we cable the line through the overflow or a cleanout. Showers that stop draining in the middle of a rinse often mean the clog is already partly open and one more load of hair closed it.

If a tub clears and then slows again within weeks, we look farther down the line for a belly in the pipe or a second blockage. A fixture that keeps returning to the same problem is not solved by repeating the same fix.

4

What the older housing stock does to drains

About 32.3% of North Chicago housing units were built before 1960, and 18.7% before 1940. Drain lines in homes that old may be cast iron, clay, or a mix of both, and they can be rough inside from decades of scale. Rough pipe catches hair, paper, and grease faster than smooth modern pipe does.

The city also has a lot of small multi-unit buildings, with roughly 1,600 units in two-to-four-unit buildings. In those, one shared stack can back up into the lowest unit. If you manage or rent in one, tell us the unit number and the floor, so we can check the stack and not just the fixture.

5

How a drain cleaning visit goes

  1. 1

    Find the problem

    We test the fixture, check nearby drains, and decide whether this is a branch clog or a main-line issue.

  2. 2

    Clear it

    We run a cable or drain machine through the line and watch the water to confirm it flows freely.

  3. 3

    Check the result

    We run water for several minutes, test fixtures on the same line, and look for slow spots.

  4. 4

    Recommend the next step

    If the line is rough, cracked, or full of roots, we show you why a camera or hydro jetting would do more than another snake.

7

Gurgles, slow drains, and the signs of a drain line leak

Not every wet spot near a drain is a clog. A damp floor beside a stack, a stain on a ceiling under a bathroom, or a musty smell with no visible source can mean a drain line is leaking. Clearing the clog will not stop that.

We look at joints, cleanouts, and the area under the fixture before we assume the whole problem is a blockage. If we suspect a leak in a wall or under a slab, our leak detection work can pinpoint it with equipment before anyone opens a wall.

What changes the price of drain cleaning

What changes the price

A one-fixture clog and a main-line blockage are different jobs. These are the factors that move the number.

Where the clog isA trap-arm clog near the sink takes less work than one thirty feet down a main line.
AccessNo cleanout means we pull a fixture or work from the roof vent, which adds labor.
Equipment neededA hand auger, a machine, a camera, and a jetter are different tools with different costs.
CauseGrease and hair clear quickly. Roots or a collapsed pipe may need a repair, not just cleaning.
When you callLate-night calls cost more than daytime bookings, and you hear the figure first.
