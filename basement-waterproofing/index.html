Basement Waterproofing in North Chicago, IL

A wet basement has a cause, and the cause decides the fix. Water can seep through a wall, rise through the floor, come in from a failing sump pump, or back up from a sewer line. We handle the plumbing side of basement waterproofing in North Chicago and tell you plainly when the problem is something else.

This is mostly planned work, and we offer same-day appointments when available. If water is rising right now, stay out of the flooded area if outlets are wet, shut off the water supply if a pipe is the source, and call. Urgent leaks are handled 24/7.

What July 2017 showed about this city

Local note

A documented storm and what came after

Lake County's stormwater plan records that on July 11 and 12, 2017, three to seven inches of rain fell across the area. A post-event survey in North Chicago found 10 flood problem areas affecting 23 properties, including 3 sewer backups.

The same plan describes repeated flood damage along the Skokie River and Route 41 corridor, tied to undersized culverts, and first-floor flooding at the Strawberry Condominiums in two incidents between 2013 and 2019. A FEMA-funded storm sewer project now targets undersized pipes at Berwyn and Bittersweet Avenue and culvert work on the Skokie River.

For a homeowner, the lesson is practical. Public projects reduce risk over time, but they do not dry your basement today. Near that corridor or far from it, a working sump pump and a clear sewer lateral are the home-side defenses.

3

The plumbing fixes we handle

Sump pit and pump

Install or replace a pump, add a check valve and make sure the discharge line sends water away from the foundation.

Interior drain tile connections

Tie collected water to the sump so it leaves the basement instead of sitting.

Backwater valve

A one-way valve on the sewer line that can reduce backup risk, installed where the setup allows.

Floor drain and trap work

Clearing and resetting drains that gurgle, smell or overflow in heavy rain.

Pump work has its own page. See sump pump repair for failures, noises and float problems.

4

Clean water in, or sewage up?

North Chicago's sewage goes to the North Shore Water Reclamation District, which states that the sanitary and storm sewers in its service area are separate and that there are no combined sewers. That helps in one way. Storm water does not share a pipe with sewage on the district side.

It does not rule out backups. Roots, grease and breaks in a lateral can cause a backup in any weather. If your floor drain overflows even on a dry week, ask for a sewer camera inspection before you buy waterproofing.

5

How we investigate a wet basement

  1. 1

    Ask when it happens

    Only in heavy rain, in spring thaw, after laundry day or every week? The timing separates seepage, groundwater and drains.

  2. 2

    Walk the exterior

    We note downspout outlets, grading toward the foundation and window wells.

  3. 3

    Inspect the pit, pump and drains

    We test the pump, check the discharge and flow-test floor drains.

  4. 4

    Rule out supply leaks

    A dripping pipe or a leaking water heater can look like seepage, so we check those first and use leak detection when the source hides.

  5. 5

    Recommend in order

    Cheap fixes first, bigger work only if the evidence calls for it.

6

Where waterproofing ends and a foundation contractor begins

We say it when it is not our job

We do not dig out foundation walls, apply exterior membranes or repair structural cracks. If a wall is bowing or a crack keeps widening, call a foundation specialist or structural engineer. If the water comes from plumbing or a sump, we can fix it.

We also do not assume every house has a basement. Some North Chicago homes sit on slabs or have crawl spaces. If you are not sure what you have, send a photo and we will tell you what we would look at.

What drives the price of a dry-basement plan

What changes the price

A new pump is a small job. A drain tile system is a project. These factors move the number.

What the source isA failed pump is cheaper to fix than seepage across a long wall.
Floor cuttingInterior drain tile means breaking and restoring concrete.
Pump size and backupHigher-capacity pumps and battery backups add cost.
Discharge routeA short run away from the house is simple. Long or tricky routes take time.
Sewer workA cleaning, a repair or a backwater valve is priced separately from seepage control.
Flood and seepage are often excluded from standard homeowner policies, while sewer backup coverage is an endorsement some people carry. Read your policy before storm season.
