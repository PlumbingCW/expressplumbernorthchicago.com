When to call an emergency plumber

Some problems can be handled the next morning. These cannot.

Call right away for water running from a broken pipe, sewage coming up through a floor drain or tub, a water heater leaking from the tank, or no water at all. Each of these can damage floors, drywall and belongings within hours, and the damage gets more expensive the longer it runs.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. A plumber is the second call, not the first.

Call the 24/7 line for

  • ✓Burst or leaking pipes. Water you cannot stop at a fixture shutoff. See emergency burst pipe repair.
  • ✓Sewer or drain backups. Several fixtures gurgling or overflowing at once points to the main line. See emergency drain cleaning.
  • ✓Water heater failure. A leaking tank or no hot water in winter. See emergency water heater repair.
  • ✓Hidden leaks. A wet ceiling, a spinning meter with everything off, or a sudden jump in the water bill. See emergency leak detection.

Plumbing services we handle in North Chicago

The most common calls are drains, water heaters, leaks and sewer lines, and we cover each in depth on its own page. For a stubborn main line, hydro jetting clears grease and scale that a cable leaves behind. When a tank is past repair, water heater replacement is usually the better use of money than a third repair.

Older pipes get their own attention. Buried lines can be inspected with a camera, and sewer line repair follows when the camera shows a break. Planned work such as repiping, tankless water heaters and sump pump repair can be scheduled around your week.

Not sure which service you need? Describe the problem on the call and we will tell you plainly what it likely is.

What North Chicago homes tend to have

The housing stock decides which problems show up.

The median home in North Chicago was built in 1973, and about 32 percent of homes date from before 1960. Pre-1960 houses often still have older drain and supply materials that are worth inspecting before they fail.

About two thirds of homes are renter-occupied, and many sit in two to four unit buildings or larger complexes. In those buildings a shared stack or a shared shutoff means one problem can reach several units, so the first phone call matters.

The city runs its own Lake Michigan water plant and publishes an annual water quality report. Its lead service line plan counts 849 lead lines and 144 galvanized lines among 5,653, which is why water service line repair comes up often.

What affects the price of a plumbing call

Plumbing prices vary because no two problems are the same. Here is what moves the number.

Time of dayNights, weekends and holidays are priced as after-hours work.
AccessA pipe behind an open wall costs less to reach than one under a slab.
Parts and fixtureA basic valve and a high-efficiency water heater are very different purchases.
ScopeA single clog, a full main line clear, and a line replacement are three separate jobs.
