2

When a leaking water heater is an emergency

A small drip from a pipe fitting can often be repaired the same day, and in many cases it is just a loose connection or a worn supply line. Water pooling under the tank is more serious. If the leak is from the tank body, no repair will hold and the answer is a new heater.

Look at where the water first appears. Wet insulation or rust on top of the tank often means a fitting above it is the culprit. Bubbling water around the relief valve may mean a bad valve or too much pressure. Take a photo and tell us on the call. We run after-hours dispatch for leaks like these, and a same-day visit is usually possible when a technician is free.

3

What we repair and what we do not

Repairs we handle on tank heaters

  • ✓Thermocouples and pilot assemblies. The usual reason a gas heater loses its flame.
  • ✓Gas control valves. Swapped when the unit will not hold temperature or cannot be relit.
  • ✓Heating elements and thermostats. Electric units often fail one element at a time.
  • ✓Relief valves and discharge pipes. A safety part that should never be capped or ignored.
  • ✓Anode rods, drain valves, and supply connectors. Wear items that matter for tank life.

We do not patch a leaking steel tank with sealant, and we will not recommend one. When repair cost approaches the price of a new unit, we show you both numbers and the data plate age, and the next step is water heater replacement. Shared building systems that run on a boiler are covered under boiler repair.

4

Lake water, chlorine, and what we look for inside the tank

North Chicago customers get water from the city's own plant, which pulls from Lake Michigan and disinfects with chlorine. The city's recent water quality reports do not publish a hardness figure, so we do not guess at how fast scale is forming in your tank.

Instead we drain a few gallons from the bottom valve. Sandy or flaky debris means sediment is insulating the burner or covering the elements, which causes noise, wasted fuel, and early failure. A flush helps a tank that is young enough. On an old tank, a stuck drain valve or heavy rust is a sign to talk about replacing it before it leaks.

What a repair visit includes

  1. 1

    Safety check

    We check for gas smell, carbon monoxide risk at the flue, water on the floor, and the condition of the relief valve.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    We test the part that matches your symptom and confirm it is the cause before we replace anything.

  3. 3

    Quote and fix

    You get a clear price before work starts. If we have the part on the truck, we fix it then.

  4. 4

    Test and explain

    We run the heater through a cycle, check output temperature, and tell you what to watch for next.

If the unit is a candidate for a leak elsewhere in the house, such as a drip in the wall behind it, we may bring in leak detection equipment to find where the water is really coming from.
