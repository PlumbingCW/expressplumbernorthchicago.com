Plumbing Installation in North Chicago, IL

New plumbing is a planned job, and it goes better when someone thinks through the water, the drain and the vent before a wall is opened. We install fixtures, supply lines, drain lines and appliance hookups in North Chicago houses, rentals and small commercial spaces.

Tell us what you want added or moved and we will give you a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow, and urgent leaks are handled 24/7.

2

Adding plumbing to a house from 1973 or earlier

The median home in North Chicago went up in 1973, and roughly a third predates 1960. That tells us what we are likely to find behind a wall. Mixed pipe materials, a drain stack that has seen decades of use, and a main supply line sized for fewer fixtures than the house now has.

Before we connect anything new, we look at the existing supply size, the nearest drain with enough slope, and the vent. If the old lines are tired, tying new work into them can mean the new fixture inherits the old problem. In that case we show you the options, including a limited repiping of the affected run.

How a new fixture gets planned

  1. 1

    Walk the space

    We find the nearest supply, drain and vent and note what is behind the wall or under the floor.

  2. 2

    Check the load

    We confirm the lines can handle another fixture without dropping pressure at the shower.

  3. 3

    Price and schedule

    You get a clear price before work starts, plus the work order and what we need from you.

  4. 4

    Rough-in, then set

    Pipes go in first. Fixtures are set after finishes are done, so tile and cabinets are not a problem.

  5. 5

    Test

    We pressure-test the supply, fill and drain every fixture and check every joint for weeping.

4

Permits, registration and 811

Local note

What North Chicago and Illinois put on paper

The city lists the Illinois Department of Public Health plumbing code among the codes it has adopted, and its building page links a plumber registration application and a contractor registration packet. Ask Inspection and Permit at City Hall, 1850 Lewis Avenue, if your job needs a permit and what registration applies.

State plumbing licenses come from the Illinois Department of Public Health, and a public lookup lets you verify one. If any work involves digging in the yard, JULIE (811) must be called so buried lines are marked before a shovel goes in.

5

Rentals and multi-unit buildings

About two out of three North Chicago households rent, and only 37.5% of homes are detached single-family houses. For landlords, plumbing installation means coordinating around tenants, shared risers and shutoffs that serve more than one unit.

Tell us when you call whether the water can be shut off building-wide or by unit, and who holds the keys to each space. Tenants should get a day's notice for water shutoffs. We label shutoff valves we add, so the next person knows what they control.

What we do not take on

Honest limits

We handle plumbing. We do not do tile, drywall, painting or cabinet work, so plan for those trades after the rough-in. For gas appliances, the fuel connection and vent get extra checks, and we may ask you to schedule a separate visit.

If your project starts with a failing fixture, toilet repair or faucet repair may be all you need, and we will say so.
