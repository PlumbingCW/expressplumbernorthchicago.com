1

First ten minutes after a pipe bursts

What you do before we arrive limits the damage.

  • 1Close the main water shutoff. It is often near the meter, where the line enters the house.
  • 2Open the lowest faucet to let the pipes drain, and open a hot tap to relieve the water heater.
  • 3Switch off the breakers for rooms with wet outlets, ceiling lights, or appliances, only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • 4Move furniture, rugs, and electronics out of the water's path, and photograph the damage for your insurer.
  • 5Call us. Tell us the pipe material if you know it, and where the water is coming from.

If you cannot find the valve, try the basement near the front wall, a utility closet, or a crawl space. In an apartment building, there may be a unit shutoff and a building shutoff, and the second belongs to the property manager.

2

Why pipes split

Freezing is the best-known cause. Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pipe splits where the ice plug sits, often showing up only after thaw. Other causes include corrosion in old steel or galvanized pipe, a failed joint, high water pressure, and physical damage during a remodel.

A burst is not always dramatic. A pinhole in copper can spray a fine mist for days inside a wall. A cracked supply valve under a sink can leak steadily. Our leak detection team locates a hidden source when you cannot see where the water is coming from.

4

Houses, duplexes, and apartment buildings

Only 37.5% of North Chicago housing units are single-family detached homes. The rest are attached houses, small multi-unit buildings, and larger complexes. In a building with five or more units, which is about 28% of the city's stock, a burst in one unit can soak the ceiling of the unit below.

If you rent, tell your landlord or manager immediately, take photos, and keep a note of the time. We can write up the break so the owner and insurers have a record. If you manage a building, tell us how many units are affected and whether there are separate shutoffs.

5

What a burst pipe repair includes

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    We confirm the shutoff works, or install a temporary stop if the valve is stuck.

  2. 2

    Find the full break

    We trace the damaged section and check nearby runs, because one burst can stress the pipe around it.

  3. 3

    Cut and replace

    We remove the failed piece and replace it with the right fittings and pipe for the existing system.

  4. 4

    Pressure test

    We bring the line back up to pressure and watch the joints for weeping before we call it complete.

  5. 5

    Explain what is next

    We tell you what drying, drywall, and insurance steps come next, and which parts of the plumbing you should watch.

If we find several weak sections or a corroded run, we will explain whether a repiping plan makes more sense than repeated repairs. A leak at the street side is a water service line repair job.
