This page explains what information we handle when you visit this website or call our line. Last updated 2026-10-15.

Information we handle

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not collect information through the site beyond standard server logs and analytics a hosting provider may keep, such as pages visited and browser type.

When you call, we may take your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem so we can dispatch a plumber. Calls may be recorded or logged for quality and training.

2

How we use it

We use call details to schedule and complete your service, to follow up on work, and to improve our service. We do not sell personal information.

Embedded Google Maps on some pages may set cookies under Google's own policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Your choices
