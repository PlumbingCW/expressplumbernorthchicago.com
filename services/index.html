How to choose the right service

Start with what you can see. Water on the floor, a bad smell from a drain, a pilot that will not stay lit, or a bill that jumped each point to a different page below. If two seem to fit, pick the one with the urgent symptom and mention the other on the call.

Urgent problems are marked as emergency pages and the line is answered 24/7. Planned projects such as a repipe or a new tankless unit are scheduled around your week. If a planned job turns urgent, the line is the same.

Unsure which page fits your problem? Describe it on the phone and we will say which service it is.
